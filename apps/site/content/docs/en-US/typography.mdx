Body copy uses the same sans stack as the rest of the site: Geist, with PingFang / Noto Sans SC for Chinese pages.

## Heading two

A short paragraph under an `h2`. Links stay in the document flow, like [Overview](./index.mdx).

### Heading three

- Unordered item one
- Unordered item two with `inline code`
- Unordered item three

1. Ordered item one
2. Ordered item two
3. Ordered item three

> A blockquote for secondary notes. Keep it quiet; the page is already dark.

## Inline and emphasis

Use **strong** for a single point of stress, _emphasis_ sparingly, and `path/to/file.ts` for identifiers.
