---
title: Dreams
description: Beta session memories. They expire after five days.
---

Dreams is a **Beta** settings page. Spirit can record short memories from sessions and expose them to the agent with `dream_list`, `dream_read`, `dream_record`, `dream_update`, and `dream_delete`.

Records live under `{spiritDataDir}/dreams/` and expire after **five days**.

Turn collection on or off in **Settings → Dreams**. A lightweight chat model is used for background collection, same slot as [Tab completion](../tools/tab-completion.mdx).

Treat Dreams as an experiment. Do not rely on them as a permanent project wiki — that belongs in [Rules](./rules.mdx) or the repo.
